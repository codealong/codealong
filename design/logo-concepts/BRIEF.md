# Codealong logo direction

Updated September 24, 2026 after the fourth exploration round.

The user wants simple, geometric marks. No 3D or overly detailed marks. The selected favorite is candidate 22, Overlap; continue with controlled variations of its layered C.

The next exploration should make the geometry visibly more precise: constant-width circular bands, straight overlap boundaries, square corners, and polygonal constructions. Keep the teal/blue palette consistent to compare the structures.

Continue exploring C and CA letterforms that suit Codealong: a Mac app that gathers coding agents and sessions across projects.

Working design criteria:

- Flat shapes built from circles, arcs, straight lines, and simple cutouts.
- Two solid component colors may create a third, darker solid intersection color for Overlap variations. The silhouette should also work in one color.
- No gradients, shadows, highlights, texture, bevels, perspective, or simulated materials.
- Few components, generous negative space, and clear recognition at small sizes.
- The letter construction should supply the character; avoid added decorative detail.

The round 4 simple geometry view selects ten existing concepts as starting points, not final approved artwork. The tactile, glass, ceramic, folded-paper and detailed object directions are outside this brief.

Candidate 22, Overlap, prompted concern about familiarity. A preliminary visual search found similar layered C constructions; its availability has not been established.
